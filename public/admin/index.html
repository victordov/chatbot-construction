                                    Why replies come from our address. Your customers' mail
                                    providers check that a message claiming to be from your domain was sent by a

                      Identity verification
                      
                        
                      
                        
                      
                            
                      
                                If your visitors are logged in, have your server vouch for who they are
                                and pass that to the widget. Without a signature anyone could claim to be
                                anyone, so unsigned ids are ignored and the visitor stays anonymous.
                                A verified visitor is who your verified-only actions can look up
                                accounts for.
                            
                      

                            
                      
                                
                                
                      
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                
                      
                                
                      
                                    Server-side only. Never put this in front-end code — anyone with it
                                    can impersonate any of your users. Both methods below use it.
                                
                      
                            
                      

                            
                      
                                
                                
                            
                      

                            
                      
                                
                      
                                    A short-lived token, signed with HS256 using the secret above. It expires, so a copy
                                    lifted from a page stops working, and it can carry details you vouch for
                                    (a plan, an account number) that the assistant and your actions can trust.
                                
                      
                                
                        
                                    
                      • sub: your user id. Required.
                        •

                        • 
                                    
                      • aud: optional; when set it must be your widget key, ….
                        • 
                                    
                      • email, name: optional, shown to your team as verified.
                        • 
                                    
                      • attrs: optional object of short values, stored on the contact and marked as signed. Anything the visitor types stays unverified.
