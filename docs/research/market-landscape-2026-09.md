# Website chat / AI-agent market research (as of September 2026)

Scope: Intercom (Fin), Zendesk, Tidio (Lyro), Crisp, Freshworks Freshchat (Freddy), Drift (Salesloft), HubSpot (Breeze Customer Agent), Chatbase, Ada, Voiceflow, Botpress, LiveChat/ChatBot.com (Text), Gorgias, Sierra, Decagon, plus three additions that are clearly relevant: Chatwoot (the open-source leader), Kustomer (mid-market CRM-style desk), Forethought (acquired by Zendesk in March 2026).

Sourcing note: pricing pulled from official pricing pages where they render; several vendors (Zendesk overage rate, Sierra, Decagon, Ada, Kustomer, Voiceflow business tiers) do not publish numbers, so third-party 2026 analyses are cited and flagged as such. Vendor-claimed resolution rates are listed next to independent figures where available; the gap between the two is itself one of the findings.

## Part A. Product by product

### 1. Intercom / Fin AI Agent
- **What/who:** Customer-service platform (Messenger widget, inbox, help center, outbound) whose Fin AI Agent is now also sold standalone on top of other helpdesks. Targets SaaS/tech from SMB to enterprise. Fin lists Salesforce, HubSpot, Freshworks, Dixa, Front, Zoho, Sprinklr and Gorgias as supported helpdesks and says it "works seamlessly with any helpdesk" ([fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing)).
- **How it works:** Live chat, support email, in-app (mobile SDKs), banners and tooltips are unlimited and free on every plan; WhatsApp, SMS and phone are usage-billed ([intercom.com/pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)). Fin answers from help center + URLs + files and runs "Procedures" (multi-step actions/handoffs); Copilot assists agents. Fin channels: chat, email, voice, Slack, social; "dozens of languages" ([fin.ai](https://fin.ai/)). Regional data hosting US / EU (Dublin) / Australia (Sydney) ([Intercom regional hosting](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6124430-regional-data-hosting)). SOC 2, ISO 27001/27701/42001, AIUC-1, HIPAA ([fin.ai](https://fin.ai/)).
- **Pricing:** Seats Essential $29 / Advanced $85 / Expert $132 per seat/month; Copilot $29 annual or $35 monthly per agent; Proactive Support Plus $99/mo incl. 500 messages; 14-day trial; startups up to 93% off ([intercom.com/pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)). Fin: **$0.99 per outcome** (resolution, procedure handoff, or disqualification), **$9.99 per lead qualification**, minimum 50 outcomes/month, no setup or platform fee ([fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing)). Intercom moved the meter from "resolutions" to "outcomes" so that procedure-then-handoff is billable ([Intercom blog](https://www.intercom.com/blog/from-resolutions-to-outcomes-evolving-how-fin-delivers-value/)). Third-party notes: WhatsApp conversations bill roughly $0.03–$0.10 each on top ([dragapp](https://www.dragapp.com/blog/intercom-pricing/)).
- **Claimed metrics:** 76% average resolution rate across 12,000+ customers, 2M resolutions/week, "resolution rate increases 1% every month", $1M performance guarantee ([fin.ai](https://fin.ai/); [Intercom blog](https://www.intercom.com/blog/from-resolutions-to-outcomes-evolving-how-fin-delivers-value/)). Independent tests report 38–53% in production ([builts.ai 500-ticket test](https://builts.ai/blog/intercom-fin-ai-review/); [clonedesk](https://clonedesk.ai/blog/intercom-fin-limitations)). Intercom's own pricing page cites a customer (Robin) at 50% ([intercom.com/pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)).
- **Why it wins:** Help-center-first data model (Fin is only as good as the articles), best-in-class Messenger UX, invented per-resolution pricing in 2023 and has kept it the simplest in the market, then decoupled Fin so it can be sold into Zendesk/Salesforce accounts.

### 2. Zendesk (AI agents, formerly Answer Bot)
- **What/who:** Ticketing-first suite for mid-market and enterprise support teams, now repositioned as a "Resolution Platform".
- **How it works:** Email/ticketing, messaging and live chat, voice, social; AI agents on every plan; Copilot for agents; Agent Builder (no-code), Knowledge Graph connectors and MCP support announced at Relate, May 19 2026; voice AI agents in 60+ languages on Amazon Connect ([CMSWire on Relate 2026](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/zendesk-unveils-autonomous-ai-workforce-at-relate-2026/)). Acquired Forethought (closed March 26 2026, reportedly >$200M per Reuters) for self-improving omnichannel agents ([Zendesk press release](https://www.zendesk.com/newsroom/press-releases/zendesk-completes-acquisition-of-forethought/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/zendesk-acquires-agentic-customer-service-startup-forethought/)). EU data residency is part of the paid "Advanced Data Privacy and Protection" add-on ([Zendesk regional hosting policy](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408883599130-Regional-Data-Hosting-Policy); [eesel summary](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/zendesk-data-residency)). Marketplace: ~1,900 apps / 1,100+ partners per third-party count ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/zendesk-marketplace-buyers)).
- **Pricing:** Support Team $19, Suite Team $55, Suite Professional $115 per agent/month (annual); Enterprise by quote; Copilot add-on $50/agent/month; AI agents included and billed "only for customer requests that were successfully resolved by the AI agent, without any escalation" ([zendesk.com/pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)). Included allowance ~5 / 10 / 15 automated resolutions per agent/month on Team / Professional / Enterprise; overage **~$1.50 committed / ~$2.00 pay-as-you-go** — not on a public rate card, reported by customers and analysts ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/zendesk-automated-resolutions); [voiceflow](https://www.voiceflow.com/blog/zendesk-pricing)).
- **Resolution definition (important precedent):** since May 18 2026 Zendesk splits "Contained" (handled, no human) from "Verified" (extra signals confirm it was solved) and bills only Verified ([Zendesk help center](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10677925692698-Announcing-changes-to-AI-agent-reporting)).
- **Claimed metrics:** "resolve up to 80%+ of even the most complex service issues" ([zendesk.com/pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)); voice AI agents "resolve 80% of tickets" ([CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/zendesk-unveils-autonomous-ai-workforce-at-relate-2026/)). Third-party reviews put typical deployments at 20–40% ([Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com/learn/zendesk-ai-agents-guide)).
- **Why it wins:** Incumbent ticketing + biggest marketplace; used per-seat cash flow to buy its way into agentic AI; outcome pricing plus a verified-resolution audit trail reduces buyer distrust.

### 3. Tidio / Lyro
- **What/who:** Freemium chat widget + inbox + Flows + Lyro AI agent for SMB and e-commerce; claims 300,000+ businesses ([Tidio press release](https://www.tidio.com/blog/lyro-achieves-best-resolution-rates-in-industry/)). Shopify app 4.8 stars / 1,338 reviews, SOC 2 Type 2 ([Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat)); WordPress plugin 70,000+ active installs, 4.7 stars ([wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/tidio-live-chat/)).
- **How it works:** Unified inbox for chat, email, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger; Lyro answers from knowledge sources and runs "Lyro Tasks" (custom flows / Shopify actions: order status, refunds, address changes) ([Tidio help: Lyro Tasks](https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/12642003268764-Lyro-Tasks-Custom-Flows); [Lyro actions](https://www.tidio.com/ai-agent/lyro-actions/)); Flows = visual no-code automation for proactive/conversion messages; mobile apps.
- **Pricing:** Free (50 billable conversations, 10 seats); Starter $29/mo (100 conversations, 50 one-time Lyro conversations); Growth from $59/mo (up to 2,000 conversations); Plus from $300/mo + usage; Premium custom with a **guaranteed 50% Lyro resolution rate and a pay-per-resolution option**. Lyro add-on from $32.50/mo for 50 AI conversations with tiers to 1,000+; Flows from $24.17/mo for 2,000 visitors reached. A "billable conversation" requires a human-agent message — AI and Flows replies do not count ([tidio.com/pricing](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/)). Third parties note the three independent meters make the real bill $68–300/mo for many SMBs ([chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/tidio-pricing); [featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/tidio-pricing)).
- **Claimed metrics:** Lyro averages 64%, peaks at 90%, "competitors ≈50%"; 50% guaranteed for Tidio+ after 30 days ([Tidio press release](https://www.tidio.com/blog/lyro-achieves-best-resolution-rates-in-industry/)); marketing also uses "up to 70%" and "67%" ([myaskai](https://myaskai.com/blog/tidio-ai-complete-guide-2026)).
- **Why it wins:** Freemium plus app-store distribution (Shopify, WordPress, Wix), five-minute setup, e-commerce actions out of the box, AI conversations metered separately from human conversations so the AI does not inflate the helpdesk bill.

### 4. Crisp
- **What/who:** French all-in-one messaging platform for SMB; flat per-workspace pricing, EU-only hosting.
- **How it works:** Website widget, shared inbox, chat triggers, knowledge base (Essentials+), workflow automation builder, campaigns, ticketing (Plus), mobile apps on all plans; omnichannel inbox covers WhatsApp Business, Messenger, email, SMS, Telegram, Instagram, X, Viber, Line, phone; AI agent draws on included AI credits ([crisp.chat/pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/)). Data hosted in Amsterdam, GDPR-first; aligned with ISO 27001 but no SOC 2 audit ([Crisp SOC2 status](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/crisp-compliance-status-with-soc2-1xet2vz/); [Crisp GDPR](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/crisp-eu-gdpr-compliance-status-nhv54c/)).
- **Pricing:** Free (2 seats); Mini $45/mo (4 seats, ~$5 AI credits ≈ 90 automated conversations); Essentials $95/mo (10 seats, ~$25 ≈ 450); Plus $295/mo (20 seats, ~$75 ≈ 1,350); extra seat $10; 14-day trial; no conversation limits ([crisp.chat/pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/)). Included AI credits expire monthly ([dragapp](https://www.dragapp.com/blog/crisp-pricing/)).
- **Why it wins:** Predictable flat pricing (10–20 seat teams save 50–67% vs per-seat vendors per [featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/crisp-pricing)), a real free tier, and EU data posture that sells itself to European SMBs.

### 5. Freshworks Freshchat / Freddy AI
- **What/who:** Messaging product inside the Freshworks suite (Freshdesk Omni); SMB to mid-market.
- **How it works:** Chat widget, email, Facebook, Instagram, SMS, WhatsApp, Line, Google Business Messages; 45+ languages; Freddy AI Agent (autonomous, knowledge-based) and Freddy Copilot (agent assist, live translation) ([freshworks.com live-chat pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/)).
- **Pricing:** Free (up to 10 agents, 100 campaign contacts); Growth $19, Pro $49, Enterprise $79 per agent/month annual; **Freddy AI Agent: first 500 sessions included, then $49 per 100 sessions** (a session = one user's interaction within 24h); Copilot $29/agent/month ([freshworks.com](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/); [Freshworks session FAQ](https://crmsupport.freshworks.com/support/solutions/articles/50000004664-freddy-ai-agent-sessions-faqs)).
- **Claimed metrics:** "up to 80%" per third-party summaries of Freshworks marketing; real deployments 23–75% ([getmacha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/freshdesk-freddy-ai-explained)).
- **Why it wins:** Cheapest credible per-seat suite with a free tier and sessions (not resolutions) as the AI meter — simpler to reason about, but you pay for failed sessions.

### 6. Drift (Salesloft) — cautionary tale
- **What/who:** Conversational-marketing chat for B2B sales (playbooks, ABM, meeting booking). Acquired by Salesloft in Feb 2024; **Clari + Salesloft announced a gradual sunset on March 6 2026 and refers customers to 1mind** ([Salesloft newsroom](https://www.salesloft.com/company/newsroom/1-mind-partnership); [salesenablement blog](https://salesenablement.wordpress.com/2026/03/27/sunsetting-drift-clari-salesloft-will-refer-existing-drift-clients-to-1mind/)).
- **Pricing:** Premium from $2,500/mo, Advanced/Enterprise by quote ([MarketBetter review](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/drift-review-2026/)).
- **What happened:** Chat-only scope, enterprise-only pricing, slower releases post-acquisition, and the August 2025 Drift OAuth breach that exfiltrated Salesforce data from 700+ organisations ([Warmly](https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/blogdrift-shutting-down-best-alternative-2026)). Lesson: a widget with one job (lead capture) gets bundled away; integration security is existential.

### 7. HubSpot Breeze Customer Agent
- **What/who:** AI support agent inside HubSpot Service Hub, for Pro/Enterprise CRM customers (SMB to mid-market).
- **How it works:** Answers from knowledge base, website pages and documents, cites sources, reads the CRM record (tickets, deals, lifecycle); Audit Cards (Jan 2026) log every action; nine channels — web chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, Instagram, Telegram, LINE, Slack, Messenger — with voice in beta ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/breeze-customer-agent); [resolve247](https://resolve247.ai/blog/hubspot-breeze/)).
- **Pricing:** Since **April 14 2026 outcome-based: 50 HubSpot Credits per resolved conversation** (previously $1 per conversation regardless of outcome); 28-day trial; Pro/Enterprise only ([HubSpot company news](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete)). Credit overage is $0.010 per credit, unused credits expire monthly ([HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/understand-hubspot-credits-and-billing)) — so **≈$0.50 per resolution**.
- **Claimed metrics:** resolves 65% of conversations and cuts resolution time 39% across 8,000+ activated customers ([HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete)).
- **Why it wins:** Zero-integration CRM context; the agent is a feature of a platform people already pay for; cheapest per-resolution price among big vendors.

### 8. Chatbase
- **What/who:** Self-serve "build an AI support agent from your data" product for SMBs, indie SaaS and agencies.
- **How it works:** Train on URLs/files/text; deploy widget, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Slack; Actions with native connectors (Stripe, Shopify, Calendly, Cal.com, Zendesk, Salesforce, Slack) plus a custom REST action; 80+ languages; SOC 2 Type II, GDPR ([chatbase.co/pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing); [Chatbase LLM info page](https://www.chatbase.co/llm-info); [Progressive Robot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/04/15/what-is-chatbase/)).
- **Pricing:** Free 50 credits, 1 agent (agents deleted after 14 days of inactivity); Hobby $40/mo 700 credits, 2 agents; Standard $150/mo 4,000 credits, 3 agents; Pro $500/mo 15,000 credits, 5 agents; auto-recharge $40 per 1,000 credits; extra agent $25/mo; remove branding $99/mo ([chatbase.co/pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing)). A credit is one AI reply, weighted by model (≈1 for economy models, ~3 for Claude Sonnet, ~5 for Opus) ([chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/chatbase-pricing)).
- **Why it wins:** Ten-minute time-to-value, product-led, model choice exposed to the buyer, agencies resell it; but no inbox/helpdesk of its own — it plugs into Zendesk/Intercom/etc.

### 9. Ada
- **What/who:** Enterprise AI customer service agent (350+ businesses: Pinterest, monday.com, Sky, Grab, Barnes & Noble, IPSY) across messaging, voice, email ([ada.cx](https://www.ada.cx/)).
- **Pricing:** Not published; quote-based, reportedly ~$30k/year entry and $100k–300k+ for enterprise, **per-conversation (not per-resolution) after experimenting with outcome pricing** ([featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/ada-cx-pricing); [fin.ai comparison](https://fin.ai/learn/ada-cx-pricing)).
- **Claimed metrics:** "84% automated resolution rate" ([ada.cx](https://www.ada.cx/)); Ada coined "Automated Resolution" as the category metric.
- **Why it wins:** Early mover on the AR metric, multi-LLM reasoning engine, enterprise SDK/API posture.

### 10. Voiceflow
- **What/who:** Visual agent builder for agencies and product teams (chat first, voice via Twilio/Vonage); white-label and multi-client workspaces for agencies ([voiceflow.com/pricing](https://www.voiceflow.com/pricing)).
- **Pricing (third-party, June 2026):** Starter free 100 credits; Pro $60/mo 10,000 credits; Business $150/mo 30,000 credits; Enterprise custom; extra editor $50/mo; 1 credit per chat message, ~10 credits per voice minute plus LLM/TTS/STT; agents stop responding when credits run out ([ringly.io](https://www.ringly.io/blog/voiceflow-pricing); [cloudtalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/voiceflow-pricing/)).
- **Why it wins:** Best-in-class canvas for conversation design, model-agnostic, agency channel; weakness is that it has no inbox — it is a builder, not a support product.

### 11. Botpress
- **What/who:** Developer-friendly agent builder with open-source roots (14.8k GitHub stars; the self-hosted v12 line has not shipped since June 2023), now cloud-first ([Boei open-source roundup](https://boei.help/blog/open-source-chatbot-platforms/); [botpress/botpress on GitHub](https://github.com/botpress/botpress)).
- **How it works:** Visual studio, Knowledge Bases from URLs/documents/web search, "Autonomous Node" where the LLM picks the next step inside a flow, human handoff, channels incl. webchat, WhatsApp, Telegram, and an integrations hub ([Botpress KB docs](https://botpress.com/docs/studio/concepts/knowledge-base/introduction); [Botpress WhatsApp](https://botpress.com/integrations/whatsapp)).
- **Pricing:** Pay-as-you-go $0 + AI spend (500 messages, 1 seat, $5 AI credit, $100 cap); Plus $89/mo; Team $495/mo; Managed $1,495/mo; Enterprise custom; AI spend passed through at provider cost with no markup ([Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/blog/botpress-pricing); [talkbar](https://talkbar.ai/blog/botpress-pricing-and-alternatives)). May 14 2026 update: AI spend is bundled into purchased conversations for new workspaces ([Botpress blog](https://botpress.com/blog/pricing-update-may-2026)).
- **Why it wins:** Developer community, transparent LLM pass-through, open-source credibility even after the pivot.

### 12. LiveChat + ChatBot.com (Text)
- **What/who:** Long-standing per-seat live chat (35,000+ companies, 200+ marketplace integrations) plus a separate AI chatbot product; SMB to mid-market.
- **How it works:** LiveChat: website widget, mobile apps, Messenger, Apple Messages for Business built in; WhatsApp, SMS, email, Instagram, X via marketplace; "Text Intelligence" agent-assist (reply suggestions, summaries, Copilot) on every paid plan ([livechat.com/pricing](https://www.livechat.com/pricing/)). ChatBot: knowledge from help articles, URLs, PDFs (50 MB), 48 languages; channels website, Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS (Twilio), mobile in-app, Shopify ([chatbot.com/pricing](https://www.chatbot.com/pricing/)).
- **Pricing:** LiveChat Starter $19 / Team $49 / Business $79 per person/month annual ($25/$59/$89 monthly); Enterprise custom; 14-day trial. ChatBot Essential $19/user annual ($25 monthly) with 10 AI resolutions/month; Growth $79/user annual ($99 monthly) with 200 resolutions/month; **extra resolutions $49.50 per 50 (≈$0.99 each)** ([livechat.com/pricing](https://www.livechat.com/pricing/); [chatbot.com/pricing](https://www.chatbot.com/pricing/)).
- **Why it wins:** Reliability and a large marketplace; note the split into two SKUs (human seats vs AI resolutions) — a common pattern.

### 13. Gorgias
- **What/who:** E-commerce helpdesk (Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce); claims to power 40% of Shopify brands; 100+ integrations; Shopify App Store 4.3 stars / ~628 reviews ([gorgias.com/pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing); [Shopify reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/helpdesk/reviews)).
- **How it works:** Email, chat, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok; voice and SMS add-ons; AI Agent uses order data to act (returns, tracking, refunds); priced per ticket, never per agent ([gorgias.com/pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)).
- **Pricing (third-party, Sept 2026):** Starter $10/mo 50 tickets, Basic $60/300, Pro $360/2,000, Advanced $900/5,000, Enterprise 5,000+; overage $0.40/$0.36/$0.32 per ticket; **AI Agent $0.90 (annual) or $1.00 (monthly) per automated resolution, and each automated resolution also consumes a ticket** unless a human is reached within 72 hours ([chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/gorgias-pricing); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gorgias-ai-pricing-complete-2026-cost-breakdown-and-guide); [Gorgias blog](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/ai-agent-pricing)).
- **Claimed metrics:** "60% of inquiries resolved instantly"; case study Orthofeet >50% automation ([gorgias.com/pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)); independent reviews put first-quarter automation at 30–40% ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/is-gorgias-ai-worth-it)).
- **Why it wins:** Vertical focus — order data in the sidebar, Shopify actions, Shopify App Store distribution, ticket-based (not seat-based) pricing that fits seasonal merchants. Its double-billing of AI resolutions is the most-cited complaint.

### 14. Sierra
- **What/who:** Enterprise "Agent OS" (Agent Studio no-code, Agent SDK for developers, Agent Data Platform) across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice and ChatGPT; customers include Rocket Mortgage, SiriusXM, Uber, Vanguard, Wayfair, Hyatt, CarMax; SOC 2, ISO 27001/42001, HIPAA, GDPR, FedRAMP ([sierra.ai](https://sierra.ai/); [Sierra Agent SDK](https://sierra.ai/product/agent-sdk)).
- **Pricing:** Outcome-based, "only pay for the value Sierra delivers" ([sierra.ai](https://sierra.ai/)); reported ~$1.50 per resolved interaction, year-one $200k–350k incl. implementation, setup $50k–200k, 3–7 month deployments ([coworker.ai](https://coworker.ai/blog/sierra-ai-pricing); [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/how-does-sierra-ai-make-money-outcome-based-pricing-enterprise-agents-and-the-business-model-breakdown)).
- **Traction:** $150M ARR by early 2026, $950M raise at ~$15B, >40% of Fortune 50 ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/sierra-ai); [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/sierra-buys-takeoff-launches-horizon-agent-platform/)).
- **Why it wins:** Founder credibility, forward-deployed engineering, outcome pricing that large enterprises accept because it transfers risk to the vendor.

### 15. Decagon
- **What/who:** Enterprise AI agents for chat, email and voice; customers Chime, Duolingo, ClassPass, Notion, Rippling, Substack, Oura, American/Delta Airlines, Ticketmaster ([decagon.ai](https://decagon.ai/)).
- **How it works:** Agent Operating Procedures written in natural language, Watchtower QA, Experiments (A/B), simulation testing, Duet agent copilot ([decagon.ai](https://decagon.ai/)).
- **Pricing:** Two models — per-conversation (default, "the majority of our customers gravitate" to it for predictability) and per-resolution at a higher rate with escalations free ([Decagon blog](https://decagon.ai/blog/pricing-ai-agents)); reported ~$0.99 per conversation and ~$0.50 per resolution in negotiated deals; median ACV ~$386k per Vendr data ([featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/decagon-pricing); [fin.ai](https://fin.ai/learn/decagon-ai-pricing)).
- **Claimed metrics:** Chime 70% chat+voice resolution, Duolingo 80% deflection, ClassPass 95% cost reduction ([decagon.ai](https://decagon.ai/)). $250M raised at $4.5B in Jan 2026; ~$100M annualised revenue estimated mid-2026 ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/decagon/)).
- **Why it wins:** Speed of implementation vs Sierra, natural-language procedures instead of flow charts, and honest pricing-model choice.

### Additions
- **Chatwoot (open source):** MIT-licensed omnichannel desk, ~33k GitHub stars, 15,000+ organisations; cloud $0 Hacker (2 agents, 500 conversations, live chat only) / $19 / $39 / $99 per agent/month annual; Captain AI 300/500/800 credits per plan then $20 per 1,000 ([GitHub](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/chatwoot-pricing)). Shows that open source + self-host is a real distribution channel for a widget/inbox.
- **Kustomer:** CRM-style desk for mid-market retail; no public pricing ("Every CX team's setup is different"), voice/SMS/WhatsApp pay as you go ([kustomer.com/pricing](https://www.kustomer.com/pricing/)).
- **Forethought:** Enterprise AI agent for support; median ACV ~$74k; now inside Zendesk ([cloudtalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/forethought-ai-pricing/); [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/newsroom/press-releases/zendesk-completes-acquisition-of-forethought/)).

### 2. The common feature baseline (what every successful product has)

Every product in the table that is growing has all of the following. Treat missing items as gaps.

1. **Knowledge sources beyond a manual KB:** crawl website URLs, import help-center articles, upload PDFs/docs, and (for commerce) product/order data. Chatbase, ChatBot.com, Fin, Lyro, Breeze all list URLs + files + help articles explicitly ([chatbot.com/pricing](https://www.chatbot.com/pricing/); [Botpress KB](https://botpress.com/docs/studio/concepts/knowledge-base/introduction)). Source citations in answers (Breeze) are becoming expected ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/breeze-customer-agent)).
2. **AI agent with an explicit resolution definition, confidence fallback and human handoff**, plus reporting of resolution rate per the vendor's definition (Zendesk's Contained/Verified split is the reference; Fin's "outcome" list is the other) ([Zendesk](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10677925692698-Announcing-changes-to-AI-agent-reporting); [fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing)).
3. **Actions / tool use**, not just answers: order status, refunds, address change, booking, lead capture, custom REST/API action (Lyro Tasks, Chatbase Actions, Fin Procedures, Breeze Audit Cards) ([Tidio](https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/12642003268764-Lyro-Tasks-Custom-Flows); [Chatbase](https://www.chatbase.co/llm-info)).
4. **Omnichannel inbox:** web widget + email + WhatsApp + Messenger + Instagram as the minimum set (Crisp, Freshchat, HubSpot, Tidio, Gorgias, Fin all ship these); SMS and voice as add-ons.
5. **Operator mobile apps** (iOS/Android) on every plan — Crisp, LiveChat, Tidio call this out on their pricing/app pages ([crisp.chat/pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/); [livechat.com/pricing](https://www.livechat.com/pricing/); [Tidio on Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat)).
6. **Proactive / campaign messaging** with triggers (Tidio Flows, Crisp chat triggers & campaigns, Intercom Proactive Support Plus, Freshchat campaign contacts).
7. **Agent assist (copilot):** reply suggestions, summaries, translation, sentiment — sold per seat ($29–50) by Intercom, Zendesk, Freshworks, LiveChat.
8. **Multilingual by default:** 45+ (Freshchat), 48 (ChatBot), 60+ (Zendesk voice), 80+ (Chatbase).
9. **Integrations marketplace + Zapier/Make + open API/webhooks**, and at minimum Shopify/WooCommerce, Stripe, Calendly/Cal.com, Slack, HubSpot/Salesforce connectors.
10. **Platform plugins/app-store listings:** Shopify, WordPress, Wix, BigCommerce — this is distribution, not just integration (Tidio, Gorgias, LiveChat, Crisp).
11. **Compliance and residency:** SOC 2 Type II (Tidio, Chatbase, Intercom, Sierra), GDPR/DPA, ISO 27001, EU hosting option (Crisp EU-only; Intercom US/EU/AU; Zendesk via paid add-on); HIPAA BAA at the top end.
12. **Analytics & CSAT:** resolution rate, deflection, CSAT, first-response and resolution times, AI vs human split, cost per resolution.
13. **Team management:** roles/permissions, departments/teams, routing rules, SLAs, macros/canned replies, tags, automatic assignment (Tidio Growth, Crisp Essentials, every per-seat suite).
14. **Free tier or free trial with no card** (14 days is the norm; Tidio/Crisp/Freshchat/Chatbase/Botpress have permanent free plans).
15. **Guardrails and observability:** off-topic guardrails (Chatbase), QA/Watchtower (Decagon), Quality Score (Zendesk), spend alerts/limits (Fin).

### 3. Distribution and go-to-market patterns

- **App marketplaces as the acquisition channel.** Shopify has 8,656+ apps across 2.17M active stores, and the average store runs 6.1 apps ([StoreCensus](https://www.storecensus.com/stats)); Tidio's 1,338 Shopify reviews and 70k WordPress installs, and Gorgias's "40% of Shopify brands", come from being listed where merchants shop ([Shopify: Tidio](https://apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat); [wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/tidio-live-chat/); [gorgias.com/pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)). Conversely, being *in* the incumbents' marketplaces (Zendesk ~1,900 apps, Intercom app store) is how Chatbase, Lorikeet, eesel, Macha etc. sell AI on top of someone else's inbox.
- **Freemium with a lifetime (not monthly) AI allowance.** Tidio gives 50 Lyro conversations once, Chatbase deletes idle free agents after 14 days, Botpress caps free AI spend at $100 — free tiers exist to convert, not to serve.
- **Sell the AI as a separate SKU from human seats** (LiveChat vs ChatBot, Zendesk AI agents vs Copilot, Freshchat vs Freddy, Intercom seats vs Fin). It lets the AI line grow with volume while seats stay flat, and lets the AI be sold into accounts that already have a helpdesk (Fin standalone).
- **Agencies and white-label.** Voiceflow's agency program (multi-client workspaces, white-label, client handoff) and Chatbase's remove-branding add-on ($99/mo) monetise the long tail of agencies that implement for SMBs ([voiceflow.com/pricing](https://www.voiceflow.com/pricing); [chatbase.co/pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing)).
- **Templates and vertical playbooks.** Lyro Tasks templates, Gorgias e-commerce intents, Drift playbooks: every SMB product ships templates so the first flow takes minutes.
- **Guarantees as marketing.** Intercom's $1M guarantee at a 65% floor and Tidio's guaranteed 50% Lyro resolution rate for Premium turn the resolution metric into a sales tool ([fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing); [tidio.com/pricing](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/)).
- **Open source as top-of-funnel** (Chatwoot 33k stars → cloud plans; Botpress community → cloud).
- **Startup programs** (Intercom up to 93% off) and vendor-comparison content marketing: nearly every source in this document is a competitor's "X pricing explained" page — SEO around competitor pricing is a proven acquisition tactic.

### 4. Pricing recommendation options for a new entrant

Market facts to anchor on: per-resolution prices cluster at **$0.50 (HubSpot) – $0.99 (Fin, ChatBot.com, Gorgias) – $1.50–2.00 (Zendesk, Sierra)**; human-handled tickets benchmark at $6–12 ([fin.ai comparison](https://fin.ai/learn/ai-customer-service-agent-pricing-comparison)); buyers complain most about *multiple meters* (Tidio's three sliders, Gorgias's double billing) and *hard stops* (Voiceflow agents going silent) ([chatarmin: Tidio](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/tidio-pricing); [eesel: Gorgias](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gorgias-ai-pricing-complete-2026-cost-breakdown-and-guide); [ringly: Voiceflow](https://www.ringly.io/blog/voiceflow-pricing)); enterprises often prefer per-conversation for predictability ([Decagon](https://decagon.ai/blog/pricing-ai-agents)).

- **Option A — Flat workspace fee + included AI resolutions + per-resolution overage (recommended for SMB launch).** Crisp-style bundles (e.g. Free with 2 seats and a one-time AI allowance; ~$49 with 5 seats + 200 resolutions; ~$149 with 15 seats + 1,000 resolutions), then $0.75–0.99 per additional *verified* resolution, with a spend cap and alerts. One meter, no per-seat tax, undercuts Fin/Gorgias on the unit price and beats Tidio on simplicity. Never count an AI resolution as a human "conversation" (Tidio's rule, not Gorgias's).
- **Option B — Per-conversation AI + per-seat inbox.** Predictable for the buyer, easier to forecast for you, but you get paid for failures; use only if your resolution rate is not yet strong enough to price on outcomes (Ada's reason for the switch).
- **Option C — Pure per-resolution with a small platform fee** (Fin clone). Best story, hardest to operate: you must ship Zendesk-grade "verified" resolution detection and dispute handling before you can bill on it.
- Whichever you pick: publish the resolution definition, publish the price (unlike Zendesk, Ada, Sierra), offer a pay-per-resolution guarantee tier later (Tidio Premium), and keep channel costs (WhatsApp/SMS) as pass-through at cost like Botpress does with LLM spend.

### 5. Five concrete lessons for your product

1. **Resolution rate is a knowledge-ingestion problem, so ship crawl-your-site + import-help-center + upload-files + commerce data before anything else, and measure it honestly.** Every vendor's real-world number lands well below its headline (Fin 76% claimed vs 38–53% measured; Gorgias 60% vs 26–56%; Zendesk 80% vs 20–40%), and every reviewer attributes the gap to knowledge quality ([builts.ai](https://builts.ai/blog/intercom-fin-ai-review/); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/is-gorgias-ai-worth-it); [Richpanel](https://www.richpanel.com/learn/zendesk-ai-agents-guide)). Copy Zendesk's Contained/Verified split in your analytics and CSAT so tenants trust the number ([Zendesk](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10677925692698-Announcing-changes-to-AI-agent-reporting)).
2. **Make actions the differentiator; your connector SDK is the right bet but needs zero-code presets.** Lyro Tasks (order status, refunds), Chatbase Actions (Stripe, Shopify, Calendly, custom REST), Fin Procedures and Breeze Audit Cards show that buyers now expect the bot to *do* things and to log what it did ([Tidio Lyro Tasks](https://help.tidio.com/hc/en-us/articles/12642003268764-Lyro-Tasks-Custom-Flows); [Chatbase](https://www.chatbase.co/llm-info); [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete)). Add Shopify/WooCommerce/Stripe/Calendly connectors on top of the generic SDK, and write an audit card per action.
3. **Price on outcomes with one meter and a cap.** The whole market moved to per-resolution in 2025–26 (Fin $0.99, HubSpot ≈$0.50, Gorgias $0.90–1.00, ChatBot.com ≈$0.99, Zendesk ~$1.50–2.00) and the loudest complaints are about stacked meters and double billing ([fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing); [HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/understand-hubspot-credits-and-billing); [chatarmin: Gorgias](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/gorgias-pricing); [chatbot.com/pricing](https://www.chatbot.com/pricing/)). Since you already have Stripe billing and quotas, implement "verified resolution" as the billable unit with usage alerts and a hard cap the tenant controls.
4. **Distribution is app stores and plugins, not a landing page.** Tidio's 300k businesses came through Shopify/WordPress/Wix listings and a free plan; Gorgias owns the Shopify support category; Chatbase and others live inside Zendesk/Intercom marketplaces ([Shopify: Tidio](https://apps.shopify.com/tidio-chat); [wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/tidio-live-chat/); [gorgias.com/pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing); [StoreCensus](https://www.storecensus.com/stats)). Ship a WordPress plugin and a Shopify app for the widget first; they double as onboarding (site crawl + catalogue import).
5. **Close the baseline gaps that gate mid-market deals: WhatsApp/Messenger/email in the inbox, operator mobile apps, SOC 2 + GDPR + EU hosting, and a per-seat copilot.** All SMB leaders bundle WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram (Crisp on every paid plan, Freshchat, Tidio, HubSpot's nine channels), all ship mobile apps, and the ones winning European SMBs lead with residency (Crisp Amsterdam-only; Intercom US/EU/AU) ([crisp.chat/pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/); [Crisp GDPR](https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/crisp-eu-gdpr-compliance-status-nhv54c/); [Intercom regional hosting](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6124430-regional-data-hosting); [freshworks.com](https://www.freshworks.com/live-chat-software/pricing/)). And take Drift as the warning: a chat widget with a narrow job and a leaky OAuth integration got sunset within two years of a $500M acquisition ([Salesloft](https://www.salesloft.com/company/newsroom/1-mind-partnership); [Warmly](https://www.warmly.ai/p/blog/blogdrift-shutting-down-best-alternative-2026)).
